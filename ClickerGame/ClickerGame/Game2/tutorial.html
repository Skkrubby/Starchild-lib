Build

Choose a class for starting stats and gear. VIG adds 5 maximum HP per point. END 10 gives 4 AP; each 2 END points above or below 10 change maximum AP by 1. Level-ups grant bankable stat points; allocate one at a time, and VIG fully restores HP when chosen. STR scales Heavy and Colossal weapons; DEX scales Light weapons.

Hands & Stances

Dual Strike requires two eligible weapons and adds off-hand damage multiplied by DEX / 100. Colossal weapons force two-handing and clear the off-hand. Two-handing other weapons adds no damage, but lets you block.

Turns & Arts of War

Actions spend their listed AP. When AP runs out, the enemy attacks and your AP refills; End Turn gives the enemy a turn early. An Art of War costs the same AP as a basic attack. Consecutive AoWs lose 25% damage each, down to 25%; another action resets fatigue.

Talismans

Equip up to two different talismans. Regular talismans have weight 0.5 in the loot pool, Epic have 0.25, and Legendary have 0.125. Drop descriptions show rarity and effect; equipped slots show names only.

Legendary: Shard of Alexander, Rotten Winged Sword Insignia, Moon of Nokstella, and Viridian Amber +3. Epic: Ritual Sword, Winged Sword Insignia, Pearlshield, and Great-Jar's Arsenal. Other talismans are Regular.

Magic & Progress

Staves and seals are spellcasting-only catalysts; they cannot make weapon attacks. Astrologers and Prophets carry four spells, and can replace one with a compatible spell found as loot. Spells use their own dice and INT or FAI scaling. Defeated enemies award XP and may drop gear. Every 10 kills brings a boss. Arcane is not a player stat.
